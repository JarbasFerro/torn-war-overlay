## 1. The rank-trigger estimate is a free, hard constraint on strength

Torn's public rank name ("Competent", "Veteran", ...) is a deterministic function of level, crimes, networth and battle-stat thresholds. TornTools and Torn PDA both decode it into a stat band, and this is the `(EST) 2M - 25M` line PDA shows on every attack page [F, both sources]:

```
RANKS: Absolute beginner=1, Beginner=2, Inexperienced=3, Rookie=4, Novice=5, Below average=6, Average=7,
       Reasonable=8, Above average=9, Competent=10, Highly competent=11, Veteran=12, Distinguished=13,
       Highly distinguished=14, Professional=15, Star=16, Master=17, Outstanding=18, Celebrity=19,
       Supreme=20, Idolized=21, Champion=22, Heroic=23, Legendary=24, Elite=25, Invincible=26
level triggers    [2, 6, 11, 26, 31, 50, 71, 100]
crimes triggers   [100, 5000, 10000, 20000, 30000, 50000]
networth triggers [5M, 50M, 500M, 5B, 50B]
stat bands        ["under 2k", "2k-25k", "20k-250k", "200k-2.5M", "2M-25M", "20M-250M", "over 200M"]
band index = RANKS[rank] - #level triggers met - #crimes triggers met - #networth triggers met - 1
```

Every input is already fetched by the overlay: `rank` and `level` come from `/user/{id}/profile` (fetched once per member for age), `crimes.total` and `networth.total` are in the same `cat=popular` personalstats call used for the energy estimate. In v2 the rank string is exactly one of the 26 enum values with no title suffix [F, OpenAPI]. Accounts whose last action predates 2016 predate the rank rework and must be skipped [F, TornTools].

Implication: **zero extra requests** give a second, independent estimate whose bands are Torn's own thresholds. The energy estimate should be clipped into the band (the band is a hard fact, the energy curve is a guess), and the plausible range collapses to the band width. This alone would have prevented the 680M-versus-2M-25M miss of v0.16.

## 2. Other public stats do not help much

- BSP's published feature list [F, archived forum OP]: attacks, activity time, awards, xanax, donator days, refills, stat enhancers, revives, LSD, boosters, energy drinks, age-non-inactive. It deliberately excludes Elo, best damage, kill streak, defends and highest level beaten.
- `damage.best` inverts to a strength floor spanning three orders of magnitude because weapon, body part, mitigation and bonuses are unknown [D]. Useful only as a "definitely above 30M" flag when best damage exceeds about 5,000.
- Conclusion: rank band plus energy curve plus observed Fair Fight is the complete public-key toolkit. Nothing else is worth a request.

## 3. Win probability versus stat ratio (simulated) [D]

Monte Carlo of 4,000 fights per point using the Attacking 2.0 hit, mitigation and damage curves confirmed yesterday (balanced builds, equal weapons and 30% armour, 25 turns, attacker first):

| Defender stats / attacker | Score ratio | Fair Fight | Win |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.30 | 0.55 | 2.46 | 1.00 |
| 0.56 | 0.75 | 3.00 (cap) | 1.00 |
| 0.70 | 0.84 | 3.00 | 0.96 |
| 0.85 | 0.92 | 3.00 | 0.79 |
| 1.00 | 1.00 | 3.00 | 0.53 |
| 1.30 | 1.14 | 3.00 | 0.15 |

With equal gear the fight is decided almost entirely by the ratio. Real gear and build variance is wider: the user went 5-3 against GatecrashR, who is at least 56% of their stats and by this table should lose every time. A logistic in stats ratio with midpoint 1.0 and scale about 0.25 (0.5 → 88%, 0.7 → 77%, 0.85 → 65%, 1.0 → 50%) fits both the simulation shape and that record, and should replace the stepped prior (0.88 / 0.70 / 0.45).

## 4. War pacing, scoring and energy facts [F]

- Target decays by **1% of the original target per hour, stepwise, starting after hour 24**; maximum war length 123 hours; at hour 124 the higher score wins. The lead is the score difference. Confirmed end conditions: target reached or a faction destroyed.
- A hit's war score equals the `respect_gain` in the attack record with every modifier applied, including the war x2. Today's RadiantRedneck hit (+3.18 at level 33) matched `1.16 × 2 × 1.37`, so the EV model's units are right.
- Chain bonus hits add their flat respect to the war score only when landed on the enemy.
- Energy: 5 per 10 minutes for donators (bar 150), 5 per 15 minutes otherwise (bar 100); attack costs 25; xanax 250; hard cap 1,000. `/user/bars` needs only a Minimal key and returns current, maximum, increment, interval and full time, plus the chain. `/user/cooldowns` (drug, medical, booster) is also Minimal.
- Hospital: Leave 15 to 30 min, Mug 30 to 45, Hospitalize 3 to 3.5 h plus upgrades. Published guidance (FF Scouter, Nuke Family): Leave offline farm targets so they rotate back; Hospitalize online or strong enemies to burn their medical cooldown, for retaliation windows, or when leadership calls locks; never Mug in a serious war.
- No public tool projects "hits to target" or an energy budget; hospital-out sorting is table stakes everywhere.

## 5. Torn PDA facts for on-screen work [F, PDA source]

- Scripts are injected on any URL that matches, including `page.php?sid=attack` and `profiles.php`; no exclusions. iOS does not inject into `window.open` popups.
- `title` tooltips never show on touch devices; every PDA-compatible script builds its own tap-to-toggle DOM panel. There is no native modal or bottom sheet handler; `showToast` exists.
- PDA's app bar and bottom bar are Flutter widgets outside the webview, so page overlays can never cover them and they never cover page content. The viewport is simply shorter. Only full-screen mode extends under the notch.
- PDA does not auto-advance after a fight. The attack page URL does not change when the fight ends; the result appears in the log list `ul[aria-describedby="log-header"]`, newest first. TECH parses the finishing line with patterns such as `X left Y on the street (+3.18)` and `X hospitalized Y (+N)`, then reconciles with the API. Reading the page you are on is within the rules.
- Faction API access for retaliation: `/key/info` returns `info.access.faction` (boolean) and `info.access.type`; `/faction/attacks` needs Limited plus that permission.
