## 1. Finding: the v0.15 strength estimate has the wrong shape

v0.15 estimates an opponent's strength as `sqrt(their training energy / your training energy)` with training energy = xanax x250 + energy refills x150 + energy drinks x15. The research shows two problems.

### 1.1 Stats are not linear in energy

Torn's gym formula (Vladar, confirmed on the archived wiki and in two open-source calculators) [F]:

```
gain per train = M x G x E x [ (a ln(H+250) + c) x S_eff  +  d (H+250) + e ]
S_eff = S                      for S <= 50,000,000
S_eff = 50M + (S-50M)/(8.776 ln S)   above (post-August-2022 cap removal)
```

The `S_eff` term makes growth **exponential** between roughly 1.6M and 200M total stats (about +1.1% of the stat per xanax at a good gym) [D], then **roughly linear again** above 200M total at about 2.2 to 2.6k stats per energy, which is the community's "1 billion stats per 1,000 xanax" rule [S] [D]. Two players both above 200M therefore do scale roughly linearly with energy, and the v0.15 estimate is close to right for them. For opponents below the cap the estimate overstates their strength (safe direction). For the exponential band the error can be a factor of several either way.

### 1.2 Natural energy is missing, and it is the largest term for the overlay's own target population

Every player regenerates about 480 to 720 energy per day whether or not they buy xanax [F, open-source energy-accounting model]. Over the 1.5-year default target window that is up to about 330,000 energy, equivalent to roughly 1,300 xanax. v0.15 ignores it, so an old-but-active account with few xanax is shown as `~EASY` when it may be as strong as the user. That is the dangerous direction. Attacks also spend energy (25 each), which the model should subtract.

### 1.3 How the incumbents do it [F]

- **BSP** (Battle Stats Predictor): machine learning on 57k spy points using attacks, activity time, awards, xanax, donator days, refills, stat enhancers, revives, LSD, boosters, energy drinks and age-non-inactive. Elo is not a feature. Best in the 100M to 10B range; ±15% is considered decent; no uncertainty displayed.
- **FF Scouter**: no regression at all. It pools observed Fair Fight values from volunteers' attacks and inverts them into a score, which is exactly what v0.14 does locally. Independent testing in March 2026 found it closer to zero error than BSP in nearly every bracket.
- **TornStats / TornTools**: rank-trigger estimator, only meaningful up to "over 200M".
- Conversion used by both BSP and FF Scouter: `total stats ≈ 0.26 x score²` for a roughly balanced build.

### 1.4 Recommended model (v0.16)

```
E_gym  = 250 xanax + 150 refills + 20 energy drinks + 150 boosters
       + 480 x active_days  -  25 x (attacks won + lost + stalemated) - 25 x revives
active_days = min(account age in days, activity time / 2 hours)   (activity.time is in cat=popular)
TBS(E) = 200M x exp(4.4e-5 x (E - E_cap))    for E <  E_cap        (E_cap ≈ 125,000)
TBS(E) = 200M + 2.5k x (E - E_cap)           for E >= E_cap
then x 1.01^stat_enhancers, floor at 1.5M
score ratio = sqrt(TBS_them / TBS_me)
```

Then **self-calibrate**: every real fight yields a pair (estimated ratio, observed ratio from Fair Fight). Fit one scale factor per user and show it in diagnostics. FF Scouter's results show that observed Fair Fight is the ground truth worth calibrating to.

Account age for every row needs one `/user/{id}/profile` per member, once ever (signup cache is permanent). The cold profile queue already exists and is disabled by a constant.

### 1.5 Win probability versus strength ratio [F] [D]

From the Attacking 2.0 hit and mitigation curves (confirmed against the wiki tables): an attacker at 1.5x an opponent's stats already trades damage about 2:1, at 2x about 3.5:1. Build shape matters as much as the total. The v0.15 tier boundaries (score ratio 0.30 / 0.60 / 0.85, meaning stats ratio about 9% / 36% / 72%) line up with BSP's colour bands (green 35 to 75% of your stats, blue 75 to 125%) and FF Scouter's "Easy up to FF 2". The tiers are fine; the estimate feeding them is what needs fixing.

### 1.6 Elo

Torn Elo is standard Elo with K=10 on a 400-point scale, starting at 1000, attack-only; the active-player average is about 1365 [F] [S]. It decouples from stats because inactive targets bleed rating. Keep it as a sanity adjustment, never a primary signal. BSP does not use it.

## 2. Finding: workflow gaps versus competing tools

Sources: Torn PDA source and issues, Torn War Stuff Enhanced ADRs and evidence, Warbuddy, TornTools PRs, CAT, FF Scouter War Room [F].

- **Attack page continuity.** Torn PDA opens attacks at `page.php?sid=attack&user2ID=` as well as `loader.php?sid=attack`. Every serious tool carries context onto that page (FF Scouter, TECH's verdict banner, Warbuddy's floating context, Native UI hospital timer). The overlay shows nothing there today. PDA-specific traps: no `#recent-history-wrapper`, no Notification API, bottom toolbar covers 100 to 150 px.
- **Unexpected transitions.** TWSE's headline feature is surfacing med-outs and early landings by diffing the API snapshot with the live DOM, highlighted only while focused. Users ask what the orange means, so label it.
- **Retaliation markers.** PDA's retal tab and Warbuddy's inline marker are the only implementations; both need faction API access (`/faction/attacks`, incoming within 5 minutes, hospitalize for 1.5x). Nothing public-key-only can see faction-wide incoming hits.
- **Contention.** CAT calls, Warbuddy dibs and FF Scouter War Room claims all exist to stop double attacks. Without a backend the achievable slice is a local "I just hit this" de-emphasis and honouring the "JOIN FIGHT" state.
- **Filters.** TornTools' Ranked War Filter (status, level, FF range, activity) and PDA's filters are heavily used. The overlay has only `ALL | TARGETS`.
- **Rules.** Staff clarification of 22 June 2026 (quoted in TWSE's repo): "pretty much any kind of highlighting can be argued to fall under drawing attention... highlighting should only be done if the window is focused." Sorting in the background is tolerated. No sounds or title changes. The overlay complies; keep it that way on the attack page too.
- **PDA robustness.** `document.hasFocus()` does not change until a tap; use `document.hidden`. Controls must not change row height. All of this the overlay already does.

## 4. EV, restated for the docs

`EV` is the ranked-war score (respect points) you can expect from one attack on that player, after discounting for the chance you lose. One attack costs 25 energy, so EV is points per attack.

```
expected score if you win = base(level) x 2 (war) x Fair Fight x chain scale
                            base: 1.00 at level 1 ... 1.50 at level 100
                            Fair Fight: 1.0 (much weaker than you) ... 3.0 (75%+ of your score)
                            chain scale: 1.00 up to hit 10, 1.25 at hit 100, 1.50 at hit 1000
EV = win probability x expected score
```

Typical range per hit: about 2 (weak target, no chain) to about 13 (strong target, level 100, long chain). Rough reading: EV under 3 is a safe farm that pays little; 4 to 7 is a good use of energy; above 8 only appears for opponents near your strength at a high chain. The number is in the same units as the `WAR +lead / target` chip, so a target 300 points away at EV 6 is about 50 hits.
