# Turn Halloween wins into a treat engine

Torn's Halloween event, "Trick or Treat", pays out per easy win. Each attack you start and win with the final hit can drop a **treat**, the base chance is **35%**, and a basket you carry from year to year raises that chance, up to **100%** for veterans. Target strength does not appear to matter ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat), [2024 One Stop Reference](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=2&t=16424442&b=0&a=0)). So the best strategy has nothing to do with war tactics. Spend as much energy as you can on the weakest players you are sure to beat, finish each fight with a "scary" weapon, and spend treats on basket upgrades rather than exchanging them early. The wiki lists the event as **25 October to 1 November**, so today (2026-10-25) is probably day one. No 2026-specific announcement was found, though, and every torn.com source was blocked, so all event numbers here come second-hand from search summaries and need an in-game check. For torn-war-overlay this is good news. The v0.23 hunt mode (`torn-war-overlay.user.js:5677`) already does the hard part, which is finding a steady supply of near-certain wins. The best additions are cheap: a live treat counter and yield tracker from Torn's official `/user/competition` endpoint, a hunt-mode finishing hint written for Halloween instead of war, and a guard so event hunting doesn't waste a faction chain bonus or ranked-war energy.

## The war meta rewards precise respect, the event rewards raw win volume

The current faction-war meta is about respect. A ranked war is won by the first side to get **ahead of the other by a target score**. Only "inside" hits on enemy-faction members count, with a **x2** war multiplier. After 24 hours the target shrinks by **1% of the original per hour**, and a war lasts at most **123 hours** ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). Chains break if more than **5 minutes** pass between hits. Milestone hits pay a bonus (hit 10 gives 10 respect, hit 100 gives 80, hit 1,000 gives 640), but in a ranked war the bonus only counts if the milestone hit lands on an enemy. A milestone hit on an outsider wastes it, which is why teams assign a protected "bonus target" ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). Experienced players stack Xanax (**250 energy** each), use Fair Fight to aim for the **3.0 respect cap**, prefer "leave" over "mug", and use hospitalization to lock out active enemies or set up retaliation, which pays **x1.5** ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). The only verified recent rule change is patch #386 (20 May 2025), which removed point rewards from ranked wars and increased caches ([Ranked War patch history](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Ranked_War/Patch_History)). Nothing was verified for 2026. The overlay already covers most of this meta with verdicts, EV, `BEST`, the chain bonus chip and the decay readout.

Halloween flips the priorities. The wiki says "any attack on any player can reward a treat". Assists, defends and losses give nothing, and you must win and land the final hit ([Halloween guide 2025](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)). Respect, Fair Fight and target quality stop mattering. What counts is **wins per unit of energy**, and a loss is the worst result: it costs 25 energy and pays nothing. That is exactly the trade-off hunt mode was built for. It only keeps targets whose worst-case strength is under 60% of yours, roughly a **93% win chance at worst** (`torn-war-overlay.user.js:5700`, `:5841`). The plain verdict: during the event, use hunt mode, not the war panel. Only switch back if your faction is actually fighting.

The event appears to be **individual**. No team scoring, leaderboard or faction component was found in any source, and nothing describes special rules for event hits during chains or wars. The faction angle therefore comes from how ordinary Torn rules interact with heavy event attacking (covered below), not from any event feature.

## Baskets compound across years, so the first treats matter most

You join by buying a **Spooky basket for $35** at Sally's Sweet Shop (level 5 or higher, one per player). The wiki says baskets go on sale the day before the event ([Spooky basket wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Spooky), [Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat)). The basket upgrades itself for free based on lifetime treats earned from attacks. Spending treats does not lower your tier, and progress carries over between years ([Torn Stats guide](https://www.tornstats.com/guides/show/48), [Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat)). The higher tiers cannot be traded or sold ([Frightful wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Frightful), [Apocalyptic wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Apocalyptic)), so "open or sell" is not a real choice.

Two more bonuses sit on top of the basket. Wearing **one** scary clothing item gives +10% (extra pieces don't add more), and landing the **final hit with a scary weapon** gives another +10%. You can use other weapons earlier in the fight. The total is capped at 100%, which a Nightmarish basket plus both bonuses reaches exactly ([2024 One Stop Reference](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=2&t=16424442&b=0&a=0)). The wiki says there are **11 scary weapons**, including Axe, Chainsaw, Sickle and the later-added Bone Saw ([Scary Weapon wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Scary_Weapon)). One guide says these two bonuses only switch on after you buy the matching tier I upgrades, **"I See Dead People" (3 treats)** and **"Here's Johnny" (5 treats)** ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat)). Next come the multiplier upgrades: Doubler (25), Tripler (50), Quadrupler (75) and Quintupler (100). Each gives a small chance to multiply the treats from a win. One guide puts Doubler at a 20% chance and Tripler at 10% ([forum 16505689](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16505689&b=0&a=0rh%3D10)). Community estimates for a fully upgraded basket land at about **1.5 to 1.7 treats per attack** ([forum 16505689](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16505689&b=0&a=0rh%3D10), [forum 16420719](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16420719&b=0&a=0rh%3D53)).

Here is the arithmetic in plain terms, at 25 energy per attack. A first-year player who has bought the two gear upgrades and reached Creepy has about a 60% chance per win, so one Xanax (10 attacks) yields **about 6 treats**. A veteran with a maxed basket gets **about 15 to 17** from the same Xanax. The first 8 treats you spend on the gear upgrades raise your chance from 35% to 55%. That is the best return you will get on any treats, so buy those two first. After that, buy the multipliers. Treats are exchanged all at once ("Exchange All") for cans, candy or alcohol, and the better reward pools are unlocked by upgrades such as Candyman, Sweet Release and Bloody Mary. Player guides are blunt: **"Never press Exchange all"** in your first or second year, because early exchange returns are poor and treats spent on upgrades keep paying ([Halloween guide 2025](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)). Upgrading and exchanging both use treats up for good ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat)). Exchange rates, market prices for the rewards, and the costs and effects of Sweet Release, Bloody Mary, Cashback and Mortal Coil were not found anywhere. Any claim about how many dollars a treat is worth is therefore unsupported for now. The top of the ladder changed in 2025: summoning M'aol to reach Apocalyptic now takes away the summoner's 9,999 treats, whereas in 2024 the summoner kept them ([M'aol wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/M'aol)). Only players near the very top of the ladder need to care about this.

Energy is what limits everyone. Guides list the usual sources: Xanax, Feathery Hotel Coupons, restaurant specials, the quarterly newsletter's +250 energy, and job points (capped at 100 a day). They give three pacing warnings. Don't spend energy before the event starts (mind TCT versus local time). Skip gym training while the event runs. Skip revives ([Halloween Energy Guide](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16394073&b=0&a=0), [forum 16511724](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16511724&b=0&a=0)). For finding targets, guides point to Donator Advanced Search for inactive players and FF Scouter for difficulty ([forum 16505689](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16505689&b=0&a=0rh%3D10)). Hunt mode already does both jobs in-house, without a third-party service.

## Faction returns come from avoiding costly overlaps, not from event scoring

With no team component, a faction gets the most from Halloween by letting members farm hard while protecting war and chain results. Two ordinary rules decide this. First, outside hits during a ranked war score zero for the war, so every Xanax a member spends hunting is energy not spent on enemies ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). Second, chain milestones count only on enemy hits, so a hunter who lands faction hit #100 on a random weak player throws away the 80-respect bonus ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). The plain verdict for leaders: if a war overlaps the event, schedule farming windows away from scoring pushes, and tell members to check the chain counter before each hunt hit near a milestone. Outside a war there is a likely upside: a group of members farming at the same time could keep a non-war chain alive cheaply, because they are attacking anyway. This is an inference. No source confirms that event hits count toward chains, so test it before planning around it.

Hitting weak, inactive players also has a timing side. Guides say "leave" gives short hospital stays (about **16 to 40 minutes**) and hospitalizing gives **2 to 6 hours** ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). In war, hospitalizing an active enemy keeps them off your faction. In a treat hunt it just removes a reusable easy win from your own pool for hours. Unless Torn rewards one finishing option over another, which no source says, **leave** is the right choice for hunting. Whether leave, mug and hospitalize all qualify for a treat is itself unconfirmed.

## Eight overlay features, ranked by impact against effort

Every recommendation follows Torn's rules as far as they could be confirmed. API use is capped at **100 requests a minute per user across all keys**. Responses are cached for up to 30 seconds. Any service that stores or shares key data must show a ToS disclosure table ([Torn API page](https://www.torn.com/api.html)). The community reading of the scripting rules is "one manual input per non-API request, and all pages must be loaded manually", and autoclickers are banned ([Scripting rules thread](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=67&t=16482084&b=0&a=0)). The official rules page could not be fetched, so that wording is still community interpretation. All the features below are read-only API or DOM work, and the user still opens every attack. The key enabling fact comes from the live v2 OpenAPI spec (v6.13.8), which was read directly. **`/user/competition`** (and `/user/{id}/competition`, public key) returns `{name: "Halloween", treats_collected, basket: {id, name}}`, and returns `NoCompetitionAvailable` when no event is running. **`/torn/calendar`** exposes the event schedule ([Torn OpenAPI spec](https://www.torn.com/swagger/openapi.json)). The repo uses neither yet: a grep for "competition", "treat" and "basket" finds no event code.

**1. Treat counter and measured yield.** Call `/user/competition` once when the hunt dock opens and once a few seconds after each finished fight. The 4-second and 15-second follow-up timers in `onAttackFightFinished` (`:6484`-`:6485`) are the natural hook. Show `🍬 1,234 (+2)` next to the energy chip, and put **treats per attack** and **treats per 100 energy** for the current session in the details sheet. That is about one extra request per fight, far below the hunt worker's ~40 a minute (`:5694`). The win and loss counts per day already live in `huntSession` (`:6216`), so the yield is just the treat change divided by wins. This is the most valuable feature because it turns the uncertain community figures (35%, 1.5 to 1.7 per attack) into the user's own measured numbers. It also covers both the "how am I doing" and "is this working" questions in one chip. Store only the counter locally, and add a line to the README ToS table, which already covers the hunt pool (`README.md`, "API, privacy and safety").

**2. Halloween finishing hint.** The hunt dock reuses the war rule from `finishHintFor` (`:2202`). An online target currently produces "If you win: Hospitalize, they are active right now" (`:6414`), and that rule exists to keep enemies off your faction. In hunt mode it pushes a reusable easy target out of the pool for hours. While hunting, the hint should default to "Leave" and add "Finish with your scary weapon (+10%)". Treat it as a few lines of text with a tooltip carrying the reasoning. It fixes advice that is currently wrong for this context, at almost no cost.

**3. Chain and war guard.** The hunt dock never checks the faction's chain or war state. Its only chain-aware path is the war-panel `loadNextTarget` (`:6540`-`:6541`), which hunt mode bypasses (`:6495`). If `getChainSnapshot().bonusNext` is true, the dock should say plainly: "Your faction's next hit is chain bonus #100: land it on an enemy, not a hunt target". If an active ranked war is cached, it should note: "Your faction is at war: hunt hits score nothing for the war". This blocks nothing and makes no extra clicks. The chain call is already paced at 30 seconds (`:88`). For factions this is the single most expensive mistake the event invites.

**4. Event window.** Sources disagree on the end date (1 November in the wiki, 3 November in an event PDF) and on the start time. The wiki's example ties the first treat to an "11 AM timeslot" ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat), [Torn Index quick start](https://tornindex.com/guide/16512766/)). Read `/torn/calendar` once a day instead of hardcoding dates. Use it to show "Halloween: 6d 4h left" on the `HUNT` toggle (`:6141`, whose title already mentions Halloween), to warn "event not started: save your energy", and to stop suggesting hunt mode once the event ends. The `NoCompetitionAvailable` error from `/user/competition` works as a second signal. The calendar's response schema was not read in detail, so check it before building on it.

**5. Basket ladder card.** With `basket.id` and `treats_collected` from feature 1, the details sheet can show "Frightful → Haunting in 37 treats" and a short checklist for new players: buy I See Dead People and Here's Johnny first, then Doubler, and don't Exchange All in your first years. Only use thresholds and costs this report marks as confirmed. Leave Freaky and Haunting's IDs and bonuses as "unknown" rather than guessing. Keep the table in one constant so it is easy to fix once the wiki is checked in-game.

**6. Outcome log.** `classifyFightLine` (`:5593`) already records whether a win was a leave, mug or hospitalize. Pair each outcome with the treat change from feature 1 and show the user a running split in the details sheet, such as "Leave: 40 wins, 26 treats; Hosp: 12 wins, 8 treats". After a day of normal play this answers, with the user's own data, the questions no source could: whether the outcome matters and whether quick fights count the same. It needs no new requests.

**7. Energy projection.** Combine current energy (already read on the attack page) with the measured yield and the time left, and show "Energy on hand: about 14 more hits, about 9 treats". Xanax cooldown would need `/user/cooldowns`, which the repo doesn't call yet, and natural regen rates were not part of this research. Start with energy on hand only.

**8. Faction treat board.** `/user/{id}/competition` takes a public key. Reading it for every member of the user's own faction, spread out over minutes and refreshed every half hour or so, would give leaders a simple standings list without any backend. It is read-only and shares no data, which fits the current ToS table. It ranks last because the event has no faction reward, so the value is purely motivational.

Two ideas are deliberately left off. A **shared faction hunt pool** would need a backend or a third-party service, which breaks the repo's "no data sharing" disclosure and would need a new ToS table. A **basket restock watcher** that reloads Sally's Sweet Shop would break the manual page-load rule, and the basket is a one-time $35 purchase anyway. Sources also disagree on the restock rate (1,000 per 15 minutes in the wiki, 100 per 15 minutes on Torn Stats, every 5 minutes in an older guide) ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat), [Torn Stats guide](https://www.tornstats.com/guides/show/48)).

## What to check in-game before coding against it

Every event fact here came from search-result summaries, because torn.com, wiki.torn.com, tornstats.com and tornindex.com all returned 403 errors. Player guides are anecdotal, and the wiki pages are undated. The only primary sources read directly were Torn's API page and its OpenAPI spec. The table lists the checks that would change a design decision.

| Unverified fact | Why it matters | Quick check |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 runs 25 Oct to 1 Nov, same format as 2025 | Countdown, feature 4 | Event banner or `/torn/calendar` today |
| Leave, mug and hospitalize all earn treats | Feature 2's "Leave" default | Feature 6, or watch the basket after a few of each |
| `treats_collected` updates within seconds of a fight | Feature 1's per-fight change | Call `/user/competition` before and after one win |
| Tier thresholds (Nightmarish at 2,500, 4,400 or 4,440) and Freaky/Haunting IDs | Feature 5 | Basket page in-game |
| Gear bonuses need the two tier I upgrades | New-player checklist | Upgrade descriptions on the basket page |
| Full scary weapon and clothing list | "Finish with scary weapon" hint | [Scary Weapon wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Scary_Weapon) in a normal browser |
| Event hits count toward faction chains | Feature 3 wording, chain-while-farming idea | Faction chain counter after one hunt hit |
| Exchange rates and reward market prices | Any treat-to-money advice | Exchange screen and item market |
| Current official wording of the scripting rules | All features | Torn rules page, or ask script-review staff (a Dec 2025 guide names Velthir) ([forum guide](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16527769&b=0&a=0)) |

## Conclusion

The main finding is that Torn's Halloween meta and torn-war-overlay's hunt mode were already lined up before anyone checked. Hunt mode was built on the intuition that volume beats quality, and the event's rules ("any attack on any player", win plus final hit, losses pay nothing) confirm it. The gap is not finding targets. It is feedback: right now the user has no idea whether a hunt session is producing treats, and the dock still gives war advice ("Hospitalize, they are active") that works against farming. Because the official `/user/competition` endpoint exposes treat totals with a public key, the overlay can be the one tool that measures the event's actual payoff for its user, using about one request per fight.

That measurement matters more than usual this year. The community numbers are second-hand, partly contradictory, and undated, and the script can confirm them from the user's own fights instead of hardcoding guesses. Build features 1, 2 and 3 first. Together they are a small change to existing functions, they fix advice that is wrong for hunting, they protect faction results, and they will settle most of the open questions above within the first day or two of play.
