# Turn Halloween wins into a treat engine

Torn's Halloween event, "Trick or Treat", pays out per easy win. Each attack you start and win with the final hit can drop a **treat**, the base chance is **35%**, and a basket that upgrades as you collect treats raises that chance by 5% per step, up to **100%** with the best basket and both scary bonuses. Target strength does not matter, and neither does whether the target is active or inactive, or whether you leave, mug, hospitalize or arrest them ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat) (read 25 Oct 2026)). So the best strategy has nothing to do with war tactics. Spend as much energy as you can on the weakest players you are sure to beat, finish each fight with a "scary" weapon, and spend treats on basket upgrades rather than exchanging them early. The 2026 event is confirmed: the wiki cites the 23 October 2026 announcement ("Don't Be a Hallo-Weenie"), baskets went on sale at 12:00 TCT on 24 October (the 2025 guide says "from 10 TCT", see the conflict note below), and each player gets 168 hours (7 days) starting at their own start slot (an 11 AM slot runs from 25 October 11:00 to 1 November 10:00 TCT). The numbers below come from that wiki page, read on 25 October 2026, cross-checked against Romarocky's long community guide ([Halloween guide 2025 (Romarocky)](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0), 700 likes, still being replied to on 23 October 2026). That guide corrected one important wiki misreading: the basket thresholds. The most authoritative source is the official in-game newspaper article Torn City Times, *Halloweek Begins!* (sugarvalves, 25 Oct 2026) (no public URL), which confirms the basket table and settles the two wiki-versus-guide conflicts. A few smaller items still need an in-game check. For torn-war-overlay this is good news. The v0.23 hunt mode (`torn-war-overlay.user.js:5677`) already does the hard part, which is finding a steady supply of near-certain wins. The best additions are cheap: a live treat counter and yield tracker from Torn's official `/user/competition` endpoint, a hunt-mode finishing hint written for Halloween instead of war, and a guard so event hunting doesn't waste a faction chain bonus or ranked-war energy.

## The war meta rewards precise respect, the event rewards raw win volume

The current faction-war meta is about respect. A ranked war is won by the first side to get **ahead of the other by a target score**. Only "inside" hits on enemy-faction members count, with a **x2** war multiplier. After 24 hours the target shrinks by **1% of the original per hour**, and a war lasts at most **123 hours** ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). Chains break if more than **5 minutes** pass between hits. Milestone hits pay a bonus (hit 10 gives 10 respect, hit 100 gives 80, hit 1,000 gives 640), but in a ranked war the bonus only counts if the milestone hit lands on an enemy. A milestone hit on an outsider wastes it, which is why teams assign a protected "bonus target" ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). Experienced players stack Xanax (**250 energy** each), use Fair Fight to aim for the **3.0 respect cap**, prefer "leave" over "mug", and use hospitalization to lock out active enemies or set up retaliation, which pays **x1.5** ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). The only verified recent rule change is patch #386 (20 May 2025), which removed point rewards from ranked wars and increased caches ([Ranked War patch history](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Ranked_War/Patch_History)). Nothing was verified for 2026. The overlay already covers most of this meta with verdicts, EV, `BEST`, the chain bonus chip and the decay readout.

Halloween flips the priorities. The wiki says "any attack on any player can reward a treat". Assists, defends and losses give nothing, and you must win and land the final hit ([Halloween guide 2025](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)). Respect, Fair Fight and target quality stop mattering. What counts is **wins per unit of energy**, and a loss is the worst result: it costs 25 energy and pays nothing. That is exactly the trade-off hunt mode was built for. It only keeps targets whose worst-case strength is under 60% of yours, roughly a **93% win chance at worst** (`torn-war-overlay.user.js:5700`, `:5841`). The plain verdict: during the event, use hunt mode, not the war panel. Only switch back if your faction is actually fighting.

The event itself is **individual**. No team scoring, leaderboard or faction reward was found in any source. The faction angle comes from how ordinary Torn rules interact with heavy event attacking, and from the fact that almost every faction runs chains during Halloweek (covered below).

## Baskets compound across years, so the first treats matter most

You join by buying a **Spooky basket for $35** at Sally's Sweet Shop (level 5 or higher, one per player). The wiki says baskets go on sale for everyone at 12:00 TCT on 24 October, whatever your own start time is; the 2025 guide says "from 10 TCT" ([Halloween guide 2025 (Romarocky)](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)). These conflict, so treat 10:00 as the earliest and check the shop. Either way you must own a basket before your start, because no basket means no treats. **Start time:** each player picks one of 25 start slots in the calendar, 10:00 to 16:00 TCT in 15-minute steps, and everyone gets 168 hours from their slot. Check TCT against your own time zone and don't spend energy before your slot. The basket stays on your account forever, needs no re-buying and cannot be traded or sold, so "open or sell" is not a real choice. It upgrades itself for free, and progression is based on **total treats earned, including those you have already spent** (Torn City Times, *Halloweek Begins!* (sugarvalves, 25 Oct 2026)). Spending treats does not lower your tier ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat) (read 25 Oct 2026)). The wiki says treats from upgrades do not count; the TCT confirms the exception that matters, Shadow of Doubt's 1,100 treats (see the upgrade strategy below). Three tiers carry an honor bar: Frightful ("Phantastic"), Terrifying ("Something Humerus") and Nightmarish ("Oh My Gourd!").

Your base chance is 35% and every basket upgrade adds 5%, so Spooky is 35% and Nightmarish (nine steps up) is 80%.

**Correction, now confirmed by the TCT:** the wiki's "treats needed" column (5, 10, 25 ... 2,500) is the amount for each step, not the running total. The lifetime totals are what you must have earned to reach each basket, and the official article's basket table matches them exactly (Torn City Times, *Halloweek Begins!* (sugarvalves, 25 Oct 2026)). The basket levels up the next time you get a treat from an attack after you pass the total, and levelling does not use up treats ([Halloween guide 2025 (Romarocky)](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)). The guide's figures also match older search snippets (Frightful 40, Haunting 90, Petrifying 1,940, Nightmarish 4,440).

Two more bonuses sit on top of the basket. Wearing **one** scary clothing item gives +10% (extra pieces don't add more), and landing the **final hit with a scary weapon** gives another +10%. You can use other weapons earlier in the fight. Both bonuses are tier I upgrades you must buy first: **"I See Dead People" (3 treats)** and **"Here's Johnny" (5 treats)**. The total tops out at 100%: Nightmarish 80% plus both bonuses. Treats can never be lost, even if you are attacked ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat) (read 25 Oct 2026)).

Next come the multiplier upgrades, each a small chance to multiply the treats from a win: Doubler (25 treats, 20% chance of x2), Tripler (50, 10% of x3), Quadrupler (75, 5% of x4) and Quintupler (100, 1% of x5). They stack, so with Doubler and Tripler there is a 2% chance of 6 treats from one win. Community estimates for a fully upgraded basket land at about **1.5 to 1.7 treats per attack**, but that figure is anecdotal ([forum 16505689](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16505689&b=0&a=0rh%3D10), [forum 16420719](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16420719&b=0&a=0rh%3D53)).

Here is the arithmetic in plain terms, at 25 energy per attack, before multipliers. The chance per win is 35% plus 5% for each basket step, plus 20% once you own both gear upgrades and wear and wield the scary items. A first-year player reaches Creepy almost at once (40%, so 60% with both bonuses), which is about **6 treats per Xanax** (10 attacks). At Frightful (three steps, 50%) the total is 70%, about **7 treats per Xanax**. At Nightmarish it is 80% plus 20%, so **100%, or 10 treats per Xanax**, plus whatever the multipliers add. A veteran with a fully upgraded basket reports about 15 to 17 from the same Xanax, which matches the 1.5 to 1.7 community figure. The first 8 treats you spend on the two gear upgrades raise your chance from 35% to 55%, the best return you will get on any treats, so buy those two first, then the multipliers.

**Upgrade strategy.** The guide's recommended buying order, numbered 1 to 20 ([Halloween guide 2025 (Romarocky)](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)):

1. I See Dead People (3), 2. Here's Johnny (5), 3. Doubler (25), 4. Tripler (50), 5. Quadrupler (75), 6. Cat in Hell (13), 7. Quintupler (100), 8. Shadow of Doubt (1,000), 9. Multipack (180), 10. Deal With The Devil (320), 11. Dark Power (285), 12. Freebie (120), 13. Bloody Mary (15), 14. Nice Chianti (30), 15. Nightcrawler (45), 16. It's Alive (90), 17. Cashback (230), 18. Candyman (5), 19. Sweet Release (10), 20. Cold Sweat (170, optional).

The guide marks four upgrades **"never"** unless you are going for M'aol: **Mortal Coil, Inflation, Save Your Tears and Summon**. Mortal Coil (500 treats) and Inflation (777) look attractive on paper but are not worth it for normal play, so skip them. Tier 2 (the candy, alcohol and energy-drink pools) unlocks at Frightful, now confirmed by the guide; tier 3 unlocks at Shocking and tier 4 at Horrifying. The Torn City Times prints the same 20-step route (Torn City Times, *Halloweek Begins!* (sugarvalves, 25 Oct 2026)) and settles the two earlier conflicts:

- **Shadow of Doubt (1,000 treats, gives 1,100):** the 1,100 treats **do count** toward your basket level, as the guide said and the wiki did not. In effect it is +100 treats for you, and it is order 8 in the route, but it takes about a year to afford.
- **Multipack:** the chance is **2%**, as the wiki said (the guide's 3% was wrong). Also, Deal With The Devil is 3%.
- **Crystalline Falcata (135 treats, a scary weapon):** since 2024 it is the only way to attack the NPCs that come with a M'aol summoning. Each one pays out by lottery: 1 relic, 100 Greater Arca Fortunae (200 to 300 treats each) and 1,000 Lesser Arca Fortunae (50 to 75 treats each). It is a gamble because it depends on how many summonings happen: 2024 had 1 (6 NPCs), 2025 had 4 (24), and a year could have none. See the M'aol section below.

Plain-language note: the multiplier upgrades can all fire at once (120 treats from one attack), but that is roughly a 0.001% event with a maxed basket, so don't plan around it.

**Exchanging.** "Exchange All" takes every treat you hold; you can't pick an amount, and the most you can hold is 9,999. Treats and upgrades carry over between years, and you can exchange at any time of year, not only during the event. Player guides are blunt: **"Never press Exchange all"** in your first year, because early returns are poor and treats spent on upgrades keep paying ([Halloween guide 2025 (Romarocky)](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)). Wait at least until you own the yellow and orange upgrades. The big payoff is the **Dark Power + Freebie + Cashback loop**: Dark Power gives +5 energy per treat and your energy cap is 1,000, so with Dark Power plus Freebie **exchange at most 180 treats at a time**, or the extra energy is wasted. Exchanging 180 counts as 198, which gives about **990 energy**, and Cashback then returns 18 treats. Because Exchange takes your whole balance, you cannot pick 180 out of 500, so exchange when your balance is near that figure. Smaller exchanges in multiples of ten also help if they fit the free energy space you have, and Cold Sweat's nerve is worth using too. Finish the upgrade route first, because cashing in earlier always costs future gains (Torn City Times, *Halloweek Begins!* (sugarvalves, 25 Oct 2026)). More energy means more attacks, more treats, more energy. The guide's year goals: reach **Terrifying (440 lifetime) by the end of year 1**, **Nightmarish (4,440) by the end of year 2** or the start of year 3, and **start exchanging in year 3**. Upgrading and exchanging both use treats up for good. Dollar values of the exchange rewards are not on the wiki, so any claim about what a treat is worth in money is unsupported. The top of the ladder changed in 2025: summoning M'aol to reach Apocalyptic now takes away the summoner's 9,999 treats, whereas in 2024 the summoner kept them ([M'aol wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/M'aol)). Only players near the very top of the ladder need to care about this.

Getting the two +10% bonuses is cheap. Wear exactly **one** scary clothing item (a second adds nothing). The cheapest by recent market price are the Moustache Man Mask (about $12k), Kabuki Mask (about $24k), Scarred Man Mask (about $29k), Nun Mask (about $30k) and Ginger Kid Mask (about $31k). Then finish each fight with any of the 11 scary weapons: Axe, Blood Spattered Sickle, Bone Saw, Chainsaw, Cleaver, Crystalline Falcata, Devil's Pitchfork, Golden Broomstick, Meat Hook, Petrified Humerus and Wand of Destruction. Only the last hit needs the scary weapon ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat) (read 25 Oct 2026)). **The cheap option is free:** Cleavers and Meat Hooks spawn on the city map during Halloweek, and you can also get them by Searching for Cash at the Cemetery. That needs a Cemetery Key (a unique drop from searching for cash at the beach) and only works when groundskeeping is inactive (Torn City Times, *Halloweek Begins!* (sugarvalves, 25 Oct 2026)). Check the map and the Cemetery before paying market price.

Energy is what limits everyone. Pacing rules: don't spend energy before your start (mind TCT versus local time), skip gym training, and skip revives ([Halloween Energy Guide](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16394073&b=0&a=0), [forum 16511724](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16511724&b=0&a=0)). Energy preparation checklist, from [Halloween guide 2025 (Romarocky)](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0):

- **Stack to 1,000 energy before your start** with zero booster cooldown and zero drug cooldown: 4 Xanax, each taken after the previous cooldown ends, the last no later than 8 hours before the start. An overdose adds about 25 hours of drug cooldown, so begin 2 to 3 days early.
- **Refills:** at least 240 points for 8 daily refills in year 1, plus 22 to 28 Xanax over the week (each has a 6 to 8 hour drug cooldown).
- **Overdose risk:** an overdose during the event is very costly; buy overdose insurance if you can afford it.
- **Newsletter freebie:** keep the 250 energy (or 100 nerve) for the event.
- **Booster cooldown limits:** a faction with the 24-hour maximum booster cooldown allows about 96 energy drinks (2 hours each) or 32 FHC (6 hours each); with the 48-hour perk, 108 drinks or 36 FHC. The guide warns first-year players won't turn a profit, so a stable economy may be the better investment.
- **Year 2 and later:** join a company with energy job specials (100 job points a day, about 800 needed; joining a 10-star company about 77 days early banks the points), save green Easter eggs (500 energy with an FHC's 6-hour cooldown), collect MCS stock energy on day 1 and again on the last day (100 energy per increment every 7 days), and consider the energy books (Ugly Energy is the guide's pick with FHC).
- **Revitalize weapons conflict with scary weapons:** a revitalize weapon restores 25 energy on the last hit (10 to 24% chance), but only the last hit counts for either effect, so you can't have both. A 10% revitalize never beats a scary weapon; from 11% it starts to win at Petrifying and Nightmarish, and these weapons cost 400 to 700 million or more. For finding targets, guides point to Donator Advanced Search for inactive players and FF Scouter for difficulty ([forum 16505689](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16505689&b=0&a=0rh%3D10)). Hunt mode already does both jobs in-house, without a third-party service.

## M'aol and the Praetorians: worth joining if you can afford it, never worth summoning

Plain verdict: join a summoning if you can afford the gear (210 treats) and have several hours free. Never summon M'aol yourself: Summon Him burns 9,999 treats and the summoner gets only an Apocalyptic basket. All of this section comes from Torn City Times, *Halloweek Begins!* (sugarvalves, 25 Oct 2026), plus the [M'aol wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/M'aol) for the 2024 history.

- **When it can happen.** The first summoning is possible at 10:00 TCT on 26 Oct, then once every 48 hours. The window closes at 23:59:59 TCT on 31 Oct, which may not match your personal 168 hours. The TCT gives this second-hand, so treat it as reported, not as a quoted rule. 2025 had four summonings.
- **Fight order.** Summoning brings M'aol and five Praetorians. Each Praetorian lands in a different foreign country, and all five must die before M'aol can be attacked in Torn City (he can then move abroad). So you travel first.
- **How long it takes.** Long. Kinzove's 2025 summoning took 7 h 13 min on the Praetorians plus 7 h 34 min on M'aol; the last 2025 one took 6 h 44 min in total (4 h 51 min on the minions); the shortest M'aol fight was 1 h 26 min.
- **Gear, bought in the basket's upgrade panel once he is summoned.** Crystalline Falcata (135 treats) is the only weapon that damages them, and Starshield Breastplate (75 treats) prevents Insanity (-99% battle stats). Both are permanent, so check your inventory before re-buying. Stygian Darkness (20 treats) gives five single-use temporary weapons: for 20 seconds, everyone in the fight can damage the creature with normal weapons. Coordinate their use.
- **Two traps.** Keep the Falcata, Breastplate and Stygian Darkness unequipped during ordinary attacks (including hunt fights), and disable temporary-item use when defending, or you waste them. And **buy everything before you fly**, because the basket cannot be opened abroad.
- **Rewards.** Lesser Arca Fortunae (50 to 75 treats) and Greater Arca Fortunae (200 to 300 treats); the M'aol armour set (Visage, Spathe, Britches, Clawshields, Hooves), permanent, with a Kinetokinesis bonus against repeated hits to one body part; and one of six relics, each lasting one year and untradeable while active: Ladso Eye (reread books), Asmol Knuckle (double damage), Sylo Tooth (+25 percentage points crit), Dyno Sac (removes negative drug effects including addiction and overdose), Nol Cloachra (reuse candy, alcohol and energy drinks, cooldowns still apply) and M'aol's Tentacle (doubles energy, nerve, happiness and life capacity and regeneration).
- **Dangers per creature.** Ladso'myna (dexterity, head hits), Asmol'yand (slows, disarms), Nol'dasaym (poison, sleep, lifesteal), Sylo'nadam (defence down), Dyno'salam (flay, gore, swallow). M'aol's wing attacks cause missed turns, his gore ignores armour, Chthonic Wrath raises his stats as his health falls, and Aegis Shield blocks 80% of hits at 1% life.

## Faction returns come from avoiding costly overlaps, not from event scoring

The guide calls Halloweek "the perfect time for factions to earn a big portion of respect": almost all factions organize chains during the event, with 50k and 100k chains as the big objectives. Members should read their faction news for the chain objective and coordinate energy with faction mates ([Halloween guide 2025 (Romarocky)](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)). That makes the hunt dock's chain guard more valuable: a member hunting treats is likely to be hitting during a live chain. With no event team component, a faction gets the most from Halloween by letting members farm hard while protecting war and chain results. Two ordinary rules decide this. First, outside hits during a ranked war score zero for the war, so every Xanax a member spends hunting is energy not spent on enemies ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). Second, chain milestones count only on enemy hits, so a hunter who lands faction hit #100 on a random weak player throws away the 80-respect bonus ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). The plain verdict for leaders: if a war overlaps the event, schedule farming windows away from scoring pushes, and tell members to check the chain counter before each hunt hit near a milestone. Outside a war there is an upside: a group of members farming at the same time can keep a chain alive cheaply, because they are attacking anyway. That hunt hits count toward chains is now well supported, since factions chain through the event on exactly these hits, but a quick in-game check of the chain counter after one hunt hit is still worthwhile.

Hitting weak, inactive players also has a timing side. Guides say "leave" gives short hospital stays (about **16 to 40 minutes**) and hospitalizing gives **2 to 6 hours** ([FFScouter war guide](https://ffscouter.com/guides/war-and-chaining)). In war, hospitalizing an active enemy keeps them off your faction. In a treat hunt it just removes a reusable easy win from your own pool for hours. The wiki confirms that leave, mug, hospitalize and arrest all give the same treat chance, so the finishing option does not matter for treats. **Leave** is still the right choice for hunting, purely because it gets the target back in your pool sooner ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat) (read 25 Oct 2026)).

## Eight overlay features, ranked by impact against effort

Status: features 1 to 3 (treat counter, Leave plus scary-weapon hint, chain and war guard) have shipped in v0.27.0. The descriptions below are the original design. v0.27.2 corrected the dock's basket thresholds to the lifetime totals (5, 15, 40, 90, 190, 440, 940, 1,940, 4,440); v0.27.0 and v0.27.1 had used the per-step amounts. Feature 5 can now be built on the lifetime totals and the 5%-per-step chance. The TCT's wording ("total treats earned, including those already spent") suggests the ladder should be compared against lifetime earned treats, which may or may not be what `treats_collected` reports (still unverified).

Every recommendation follows Torn's rules as far as they could be confirmed. API use is capped at **100 requests a minute per user across all keys**. Responses are cached for up to 30 seconds. Any service that stores or shares key data must show a ToS disclosure table ([Torn API page](https://www.torn.com/api.html)). The community reading of the scripting rules is "one manual input per non-API request, and all pages must be loaded manually", and autoclickers are banned ([Scripting rules thread](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=67&t=16482084&b=0&a=0)). The official rules page could not be fetched, so that wording is still community interpretation. All the features below are read-only API or DOM work, and the user still opens every attack. The key enabling fact comes from the live v2 OpenAPI spec (v6.13.8), which was read directly. **`/user/competition`** (and `/user/{id}/competition`, public key) returns `{name: "Halloween", treats_collected, basket: {id, name}}`, and returns `NoCompetitionAvailable` when no event is running. **`/torn/calendar`** exposes the event schedule ([Torn OpenAPI spec](https://www.torn.com/swagger/openapi.json)). The repo uses neither yet: a grep for "competition", "treat" and "basket" finds no event code.

**1. Treat counter and measured yield.** Call `/user/competition` once when the hunt dock opens and once a few seconds after each finished fight. The 4-second and 15-second follow-up timers in `onAttackFightFinished` (`:6484`-`:6485`) are the natural hook. Show `🍬 1,234 (+2)` next to the energy chip, and put **treats per attack** and **treats per 100 energy** for the current session in the details sheet. That is about one extra request per fight, far below the hunt worker's ~40 a minute (`:5694`). The win and loss counts per day already live in `huntSession` (`:6216`), so the yield is just the treat change divided by wins. This is the most valuable feature because it turns the uncertain community figures (35%, 1.5 to 1.7 per attack) into the user's own measured numbers. It also covers both the "how am I doing" and "is this working" questions in one chip. Store only the counter locally, and add a line to the README ToS table, which already covers the hunt pool (`README.md`, "API, privacy and safety").

**2. Halloween finishing hint.** The hunt dock reuses the war rule from `finishHintFor` (`:2202`). An online target currently produces "If you win: Hospitalize, they are active right now" (`:6414`), and that rule exists to keep enemies off your faction. In hunt mode it pushes a reusable easy target out of the pool for hours. While hunting, the hint should default to "Leave" and add "Finish with your scary weapon (+10%)". Attack type does not affect treats, so the "Leave" part is only about getting the target back sooner. Treat it as a few lines of text with a tooltip carrying the reasoning. It fixes advice that is currently wrong for this context, at almost no cost.

**3. Chain and war guard.** The hunt dock never checks the faction's chain or war state. Its only chain-aware path is the war-panel `loadNextTarget` (`:6540`-`:6541`), which hunt mode bypasses (`:6495`). If `getChainSnapshot().bonusNext` is true, the dock should say plainly: "Your faction's next hit is chain bonus #100: land it on an enemy, not a hunt target". If an active ranked war is cached, it should note: "Your faction is at war: hunt hits score nothing for the war". This blocks nothing and makes no extra clicks. The chain call is already paced at 30 seconds (`:88`). For factions this is the single most expensive mistake the event invites.

**4. Event window.** The window is per player: each player picks one of 25 start slots (10:00 to 16:00 TCT, every 15 minutes) and gets 168 hours from it (an 11 AM slot ends 1 November 10:00 TCT) ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat) (read 25 Oct 2026), [Halloween guide 2025 (Romarocky)](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)). The slot is the player's own choice, so the script needs either `/torn/calendar` to expose it or a manual setting. Read `/torn/calendar` once a day instead of hardcoding dates. Use it to show "Halloween: 6d 4h left" on the `HUNT` toggle (`:6141`, whose title already mentions Halloween), to warn "event not started: save your energy", and to stop suggesting hunt mode once the event ends. The `NoCompetitionAvailable` error from `/user/competition` works as a second signal. The calendar's response schema was not read in detail, so check it before building on it.

**5. Basket ladder card.** With `basket.id` and `treats_collected` from feature 1, the details sheet can show "Frightful → Haunting in 37 treats" and a short checklist for new players: buy I See Dead People and Here's Johnny first, then Doubler, and don't Exchange All in your first years. Use the lifetime totals (5, 15, 40, 90, 190, 440, 940, 1,940, 4,440), not the per-step amounts (5, 10, 25 ... 2,500), and the chance of 35% plus 5% per step. The TCT says progression uses total treats earned including spent ones, but whether `treats_collected` is that lifetime figure or a spendable balance is still unverified, which matters for this card. Add the guide's year goals as a hint: Terrifying (440) after year 1, Nightmarish (4,440) after year 2. Item IDs for Freaky, Haunting, Shocking and Terrifying stay unknown, so key the card on treat counts, not IDs. Keep the table in one constant.

**6. Outcome log.** `classifyFightLine` (`:5593`) already records whether a win was a leave, mug or hospitalize. Pair each outcome with the treat change from feature 1 and show the user a running split in the details sheet, such as "Leave: 40 wins, 26 treats; Hosp: 12 wins, 8 treats". The wiki already says the outcome does not change the treat chance, so this log is now only a sanity check of that claim and of the real yield. It needs no new requests.

**7. Energy projection.** Combine current energy (already read on the attack page) with the measured yield and the time left, and show "Energy on hand: about 14 more hits, about 9 treats". Xanax cooldown would need `/user/cooldowns`, which the repo doesn't call yet, and natural regen rates were not part of this research. Start with energy on hand only.

**8. Faction treat board.** `/user/{id}/competition` takes a public key. Reading it for every member of the user's own faction, spread out over minutes and refreshed every half hour or so, would give leaders a simple standings list without any backend. It is read-only and shares no data, which fits the current ToS table. It ranks last because the event has no faction reward, so the value is purely motivational.

**New candidates from the 2025 guide**, ranked below the shipped items (1 to 3) and below the basket card:

- **(a) Event-window chip.** Show time left in the player's own 168-hour window. It needs the player's start slot, from `/torn/calendar` if it exposes it, otherwise a manual setting. This refines feature 4.
- **(b) Exchange planner in the tools sheet.** Show the Dark Power sweet spot (at most 180 treats per exchange, roughly 990 energy with Freebie) once the API reports a spendable treat count. It cannot be built safely until we know whether `treats_collected` is lifetime or spendable, which is still unverified.
- **(c) M'aol gear reminder on the hunt dock.** A low-cost idea, ranked below everything above: remind the user to keep Stygian Darkness and the other M'aol gear (Falcata, Breastplate) unequipped on hunt fights, since equipped temporary items are wasted on ordinary attacks. Show it only once a summoning is active. How to detect an active summoning is unverified, so check what the API or page exposes before building.

Two ideas are deliberately left off. A **shared faction hunt pool** would need a backend or a third-party service, which breaks the repo's "no data sharing" disclosure and would need a new ToS table. A **basket restock watcher** that reloads Sally's Sweet Shop would break the manual page-load rule, and the basket is a one-time $35 purchase anyway. Baskets restock 1,000 every 5 minutes according to the latest patch note ([Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat) (read 25 Oct 2026); the page's intro text still says every 15 minutes).

## Conclusion

The main finding is that Torn's Halloween meta and torn-war-overlay's hunt mode were already lined up before anyone checked. Hunt mode was built on the intuition that volume beats quality, and the event's rules ("any attack on any player", win plus final hit, losses pay nothing) confirm it. The gap is not finding targets. It is feedback: right now the user has no idea whether a hunt session is producing treats, and the dock still gives war advice ("Hospitalize, they are active") that works against farming. Because the official `/user/competition` endpoint exposes treat totals with a public key, the overlay can be the one tool that measures the event's actual payoff for its user, using about one request per fight.

That measurement still matters. The wiki gives the chance per win, but the real yield with multipliers is only known through community estimates, and the script can measure it from the user's own fights. Features 1, 2 and 3 have shipped in v0.27.0. They fix advice that was wrong for hunting, protect faction results, and will settle the remaining open questions above within the first day or two of play. Feature 5 (basket progress) is the next sensible build.
