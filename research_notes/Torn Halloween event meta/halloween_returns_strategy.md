# Torn Halloween event returns strategy (2025-2026)

Research limits: WebSearch returned mostly Torn wiki basket pages (as search-tool summaries). Direct fetches of wiki.torn.com returned HTTP 403. I found no forum threads, Reddit posts, price-tracker data, Greasy Fork Halloween scripts or TornTools/PDA changelogs. Most of the requested playbook is therefore a gap, not a finding. Wiki pages are undated, so year labels are only given where the page itself states one.

## Most efficient way to farm treats/baskets/score

### Takeaway
The only solid evidence is the basket progression: a basket adds a percentage to the chance of getting a treat when you attack, and the bonus rises with tier. Nothing citable on energy per treat, target selection, or xanax/refill timing.

### Cited Findings
- Spooky is the entry basket: $35 from Sally's Sweet Shop during the event, stock respawns every 5 minutes, one basket per player. It upgrades to Creepy after 5 treats. Circulation about 16,044. — [Spooky](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Spooky)
- Creepy gives +5% treat chance; Frightful (Freaky basket at 40 total treats) gives +15% when attacking. — [Creepy](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Creepy), [Frightful](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Frightful)
- Frightful upgrades to Haunting at 90 total treats (per a search summary of the Frightful page; not confirmed by direct fetch). — [Frightful](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Frightful)
- Horrifying (Haunting basket at 1,000 total treats) gives +35%; Petrifying gives +40% (1,940 total treats). Circulation roughly 5,815 and 6,161. — [Horrifying](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Horrifying), [Petrifying](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Petrifying)
- Nightmarish gives +45%; reached after 4,440 total treats. The Petrifying page says 2,500 additional treats (4,400 total), so the wiki is inconsistent by 40. — [Nightmarish](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Nightmarish), [Petrifying](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Petrifying)
- Apocalyptic is the top tier, needs 9,999 treats, boost is unknown but under 100%, and only two players own one (Proxima, Heasley4Hemp). — [Apocalyptic](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Apocalyptic)
- The Apocalyptic page says that in 2024 Proxima kept his treats after summoning M'aol, but in 2025 M'aol "reclaimed 9999 treats" from his basket. This is lore-style wording and I could not verify the mechanic. — [Apocalyptic](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Apocalyptic)

### Inferences
- The basket tier is a multiplier on treats per attack, so buying the $35 Spooky basket on day one and keeping it is the obvious baseline. The treat count only helps if treats convert into rewards, which I could not confirm.
- Treats-per-energy depends on attack energy cost (25 energy per attack in standard Torn) combined with the treat chance. I did not find the base chance, so I cannot give a per-energy yield.

### Gaps
- Base treat chance, treat types/rewards, and any score or merit mapping were not found.
- Target selection (inactive or low-level targets, target lists), refill, xanax and energy drink timing: no sources found.
- 2025 and 2026 event dates and any rule changes: not found.

## Open baskets vs sell; market prices and trends

### Takeaway
The upgradeable baskets seem to be untradeable and are not meant to be sold. No market price data was found for any Halloween item.

### Cited Findings
- Frightful and Petrifying baskets cannot be traded, sold to a store, or found via city finds. Apocalyptic cannot be sold on the item market, traded, sent or dumped. — [Frightful](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Frightful), [Petrifying](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Petrifying), [Apocalyptic](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Apocalyptic)
- Higher tiers cannot be bought; only the $35 Spooky basket is purchasable. — [Spooky](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Spooky)

### Inferences
- "Open or sell" is probably not a choice for the baskets themselves. It may apply to treat-reward items, which I did not identify.

### Gaps
- Prices and before/during/after-event trends for candy, masks, pumpkin items and plushies: no data found (no tornexchange, YATA or item market history reached).
- What "opening" yields, if anything: not found.
