# Torn City Times, "Halloweek Begins!" by sugarvalves, 25/10/26 (official in-game newspaper)

Transcribed from 13 phone screenshots the user sent on 2026-10-25. This is the most authoritative 2026 source so far and settles several conflicts between the wiki and the 2025 forum guide.

## Basket (confirms v0.27.2)
- Spooky starts at 35%; each basket adds 5 percentage points. Progression is free and **based on total treats earned, including those already spent**.
- Table "Halloween basket progression", total treats earned / base chance: Spooky 0 / 35%, Creepy 5 / 40%, Freaky 15 / 45%, Frightful 40 / 50%, Haunting 90 / 55%, Shocking 190 / 60%, Terrifying 440 / 65%, Horrifying 940 / 70%, Petrifying 1,940 / 75%, Nightmarish 4,440 / 80%.
- Upgrade tiers open at Frightful, Shocking and Horrifying (tier 1 immediately). Honours: Phantastic (Frightful), Something Humerus (Terrifying), Oh My Gourd! (Nightmarish).
- Scary clothing and weapons can bring Nightmarish to 100%.

## Upgrade order (Andyman's route, as printed)
1 I See Dead People 3 · 2 Here's Johnny 5 · 3 Doubler 25 (20%) · 4 Tripler 50 (10%) · 5 Quadrupler 75 (5%) · 6 Cat in Hell 13 · 7 Quintupler 100 (1%) · 8 Shadow of Doubt 1,000 (grants 1,100) · 9 Multipack 180 (**2%**) · 10 Deal with the Devil 320 (3%) · 11 Dark Power 285 · 12 Freebie 120 · 13 Bloody Mary 15 · 14 Nice Chianti 30 · 15 Nightcrawler 45 · 16 It's Alive 90 · 17 Cashback 230 · 18 Candyman 5 · 19 Sweet Release 10 · 20 Cold Sweat 170.
- Multipliers stack: Doubler and Tripler together give 6 treats.
- **Shadow of Doubt: effectively +100 treats, and the 1,100 count toward basket progression** (settles the guide-vs-wiki conflict in favour of the guide).
- Save Your Tears (95, +500 happy per treat exchanged) is optional, only useful for exchanging outside Halloweek before a gym session.
- Mortal Coil (500, 1 treat/hour) and Inflation (777, +0.1%/hour on the balance) are for players saving toward a summoning. Mortal Coil won't repay its price in a single event, and Inflation does little if you empty the basket regularly.

## Exchanging
- Finish the upgrade route first; cashing in earlier always costs future gains.
- With Freebie, exchanging 180 gives an effective 198; with Dark Power that is 990 energy; Cashback returns 18 treats.
- **Exchange at most 180 at a time** or the energy is wasted (1,000 energy cap). Exchange takes the whole balance, so you can't pick 180 from 500. Smaller exchanges in multiples of ten help if they fit your free energy space. Use the Cold Sweat nerve too.

## M'aol (summoning)
- Summon Him (tier IV) consumes 9,999 treats and brings M'aol plus five Praetorian minions. The summoner gets only an Apocalyptic basket.
- Second-hand rule: first summoning possible at **10:00 TCT on 26 Oct**, then once every **48 hours**. Summoning window closes **23:59:59 TCT on 31 Oct**, which may not match your personal event end.
- 2025 had four summonings (Proxima first in 2024; Heasleys4hemp on 26 Oct 2025).
- The five Praetorians each land in a different foreign country and must all die before M'aol can be attacked in Torn City (he can then move abroad). Ladso'myna (dexterity, head hits), Asmol'yand (slows, disarms), Nol'dasaym (poison, sleep, lifesteal), Sylo'nadam (defence down), Dyno'salam (flay, gore, swallow). M'aol: wing attacks cause missed turns, gore ignores armour, Chthonic Wrath raises stats as health falls, Aegis Shield blocks 80% of hits at 1% life.
- Durations: Kinzove's 2025 summoning took 7 h 13 min for the Praetorians plus 7 h 34 min for M'aol; the last 2025 summoning took 6 h 44 min total (4 h 51 min on minions); the shortest M'aol fight was 1 h 26 min.

## Equipment (bought in the basket's upgrade panel once M'aol is summoned)
- Crystalline Falcata, 135 treats: damages M'aol and Praetorians despite their protection from conventional weapons.
- Starshield Breastplate, 75 treats: prevents Insanity (-99% battle stats).
- Both are permanent; check your inventory before re-buying (210 treats together).
- Stygian Darkness, 20 treats for five single-use temporary weapons: for 20 seconds everyone in the fight can damage the creature with normal weapons (was 5 s before 2025). Coordinate their use.
- **Keep these unequipped during ordinary attacks and disable temporary-item use when defending**, or you waste them. Buy everything before flying, because the basket can't be opened abroad.

## Rewards
- Lesser Arca Fortunae (50–75 treats), Greater Arca Fortunae (200–300).
- M'aol armour: Visage, Spathe, Britches, Clawshields, Hooves (head, body, legs, hands, feet); Kinetokinesis bonus reduces damage from repeated hits to the same body part; permanent.
- One relic per creature, power lasts one year, untradeable while active: Ladso Eye (reread books), Asmol Knuckle (double damage), Sylo Tooth (+25 pp crit), Dyno Sac (removes negative drug effects incl. addiction and overdose), Nol Cloachra (reuse candy, alcohol, energy drinks; cooldowns still apply), M'aol's Tentacle (doubles energy, nerve, happiness and life capacity and regen).

## Free scary weapons
- Cleavers and Meat Hooks can spawn on the city map during Halloweek.
- Also from Search for Cash at the Cemetery (needs a Cemetery Key, a unique drop from searching for cash at the beach), only when groundskeeping is inactive.
