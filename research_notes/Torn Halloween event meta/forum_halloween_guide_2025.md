## Event ("Halloweek")
- Runs 25 Oct to 1 Nov. Each player picks a start time in the "calendar" section: 25 slots from 10:00 to 16:00 TCT, every 15 minutes. Everyone gets 168 hours.
- Buy the basket first (no basket, no treats): from 10 TCT at Sally's Sweet Shop, $35, level 5; restocked 1,000 every 15 minutes (the guide's figure).
- Check TCT against your own time zone; don't spend energy before your start.
- You only earn treats if you win and land the last hit. Defeats and assists give nothing. The guide suggests attacking offline or inactive players.
- Don't train or revive with energy during the event. Don't exchange treats in the first year.

## Energy preparation
- First year: at least 240 points for 8 daily refills, 22–28 Xanax (6–8 h drug cooldown each), overdose insurance if affordable (an OD in Halloweek is very costly).
- If affordable: 96 energy drinks (2 h booster cooldown each) or 32 FHC (6 h) for a faction with a 24 h max booster cooldown; 108 drinks or 36 FHC with the 48 h perk. The guide warns first-year players won't make money, so a stable economy may be the better investment.
- Save the newsletter freebie (250 energy or 100 nerve) for the event.
- Stack 1,000 energy before the start with 0 booster and 0 drug cooldown: 4 Xanax, each after the previous cooldown ends; the last no later than 8 h before the start. An overdose gives ~25 h drug cooldown, so start stacking 2–3 days early.
- Second year and later: join a company with energy job specials (100 job points a day max, ~800 needed for the event; candle shop 5 E/jp at 7★, farm 7 E/jp at 10★, furniture store 3 E, game shop 5 E + nerve + happy, pub 3 E, restaurant 3 E plus 25% consumable cooldown reduction at 10★, grocery store consumable cooldown and gain, TV network). Joining a 10★ company ~77 days before the event banks the points.
- Green Easter eggs: 500 energy with an FHC's 6 h booster cooldown; save them for Halloween.
- Red Cow / Rockstar Rudolph ~$2.2m each (38 E with the Voracity energy-drink perk); FHC ~$14m. Prices are lowest after Christmas and highest in September–October.
- MCS stock: 100 energy every 7 days per increment; collect on day 1 and again on the last day.
- Books: Fuelling Your Way To Failure (double energy-drink effect, 31 days), Ugly Energy (max energy 250, the guide's pick with FHC), Self Control Is For Losers (halves consumable cooldowns), Memories and Mammaries (repeats the last book).
- Revitalize weapons (restore 25 E on the last hit, 10–24% chance) cannot be combined with the scary-weapon bonus because only the last hit counts. A 10% revitalize is never better than a scary weapon; from 11% it starts to win at Petrifying/Nightmarish. Very expensive (400–700M+).

## Upgrades: recommended order (Andyman's numbering, IceBlueFire's table)
Tier 1 (cumulative 258): 1 I See Dead People (3), 2 Here's Johnny (5), 3 Doubler (25, 20%), 4 Tripler (50, 10%), 5 Quadrupler (75, 5%), 7 Quintupler (100, 1%).
Tier 2, unlocks at Frightful (cumulative 453 for the tier): 18 Candyman (5), 19 Sweet Release (10), 13 Bloody Mary (15), 14 Nice Chianti (30), 15 Nightcrawler (45), 16 It's Alive (90).
Tier 3, unlocks at Shocking (1,366 total so far): 6 Cat in Hell (13), never Save Your Tears (95), 12 Freebie (120, one bonus treat per 10 exchanged), 20 Cold Sweat (170, optional), 17 Cashback (230), 11 Dark Power (285, +5 energy per treat exchanged, up to 1,000 energy).
Tier 4, unlocks at Horrifying: 9 Multipack (180, the guide says 3%; the wiki says 2%), 10 Deal with the Devil (320, 3%), Mortal Coil (500) and Inflation (777) marked "never unless you want to summon M'aol", 8 Shadow of Doubts (1,000, gives 1,100 treats), Summon (9,999) "never". Grand total 14,142.
- The guide says Shadow of Doubts' 1,100 treats count towards the basket level. The wiki says treats from upgrades do not. Conflicting; unverified.
- Doubler, Tripler, Quadrupler and Quintupler can all trigger together: 120 treats in one attack, ~0.001% chance with a maxed basket.
- Crystalline Falcata: a scary weapon bought for 135 treats ("upgrade 7.5"). Since 2024 it lets you attack the NPCs summoned with M'aol. Each minion gives, by lottery, 1 relic, 100 Greater Arca Fortunae (200–300 treats each) and 1,000 Lesser Arca Fortunae (50–75 treats). 2024 had 1 summoning (6 NPCs); 2025 had 4 (24). There could be none in a year, so it's a gamble.

## Exchanging
- "Exchange All" uses every treat you hold; you can't pick an amount. Maximum held is 9,999. Treats and upgrades carry over between years, and you can exchange at any time of year, not only during the event.
- Wait at least until you have all the yellow and orange upgrades. Shadow of Doubts takes about a year to afford.
- Dark Power: 5 energy per treat, max 1,000 energy, so exchange at no more than 200 treats. With Freebie, exchanging at 180 counts as 198 treats, giving 990 energy. Cashback then returns 18 treats to the basket. The guide's loop: more energy → more attacks → more treats → more energy.
- Year goals: end of year 1, Terrifying; end of year 2 or start of year 3, Nightmarish; year 3, start exchanging.

## Factions and chains
- "Halloweek is the perfect time for factions to earn a big portion of respect": almost all factions organize chains during the event; 50k and 100k chains are the big objectives. Read your faction's news for the chain objective, and coordinate energy with faction mates.
