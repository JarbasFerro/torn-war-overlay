# Torn City Halloween event mechanics (2026 vs 2023-2025)

Method note: torn.com, wiki.torn.com, tornindex.com and tornstats.com returned 403/Cloudflare to direct fetch. All findings below come from search-result summaries of those pages, not full-page reads. Exact numbers (upgrade costs, drop odds, exchange rates) were not retrievable and should be verified in-game or on the wiki. Nothing found is explicitly dated "2026"; the wiki's Trick or Treat page is evergreen and is the only source for the current dates.

## What are the 2026 dates and is the format the same as 2025? What changed versus previous years?

### Takeaway
The event is "Trick or Treat", and the wiki lists a fixed annual window of Oct 25 - Nov 1. No source explicitly confirms 2026 dates or any 2026 format change; the best evidence is that the format is the same as 2025, a basket-and-treats system that has run for several years. I found no 2026 patch notes.

### Cited Findings
- The wiki lists the event as Oct 25 - Nov 1, with treats earned by defeating other players and spent on basket upgrades and prizes. Its example is that with an 11 AM timeslot the first treat proc is 25 Oct 11:00 TCT and the last is 1 Nov 10:00 TCT — [Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat), [Current events wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Torncity_WIKI_-_The_official_help_and_support_guide:Current_events)
- Torn's own Facebook post from an earlier year says baskets were available from Sally's Sweet Shop "until noon on November 1st" and told players to "dress up" — [Torn Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/tornrpg/photos/halloween-baskets-are-now-available-from-sallys-sweet-shop-you-have-until-noon-o/10155697050341073/)
- A Torn Index "Halloween Quick Start Guide" (thread 16512766, shown as posted a few days before today) describes the event as Oct 25 - Nov 1 with a $35 basket — [Torn Index](https://tornindex.com/guide/16512766/). Its year is not confirmed, but the thread ID is higher than the Sept 2025 guide's (16504610), which suggests it is a 2025 or later post.
- Player forum guide "Halloween guide 2025 - 1st, 2nd and 3rd year" (posted Sept 2025) covers per-player-year progression — [Torn forums](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0). A "Trick or Treat Upgrades Guide 1-4 Years" also exists — [Torn forums](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16301904&b=0&a=0)
- Year-over-year changes found: in 2024 treats were kept after summoning M'aol (summoner Proxima); in 2025 M'aol "posthumously reclaimed" 9,999 treats from the summoner's basket — [M'aol wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/M'aol), [Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat)
- 2023 and 2024 had a separate "Costume Torn to Shreds" feature in which Enchanted Pumpkin candy drops were replaced with costume pieces — [Domo fandom wiki](https://domo.fandom.com/wiki/Costume_Torn_to_Shreds). This is from a fan wiki and is not verified; one search summary said it is not part of the current Trick or Treat event.
- Older guides give a different event start (one says Oct 27) and different restock timing — see search summaries of [Torn forums 2023 guide](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16192843&b=0&a=0rh%3D42)

### Inferences
- Because the wiki states fixed dates, 2026 is probably Oct 25 - Nov 1 (today, 2026-10-25, would be day one). This is unconfirmed.
- No sign of a team/competition format; the event is individual.

### Gaps
- No 2026-specific announcement, patch note or forum post found. Whether anything changed from 2025 is unknown.
- Torn Index guide year is unconfirmed.
- Exact end time (noon Nov 1 TCT in the older Facebook post vs the wiki's timeslot-based last proc) not reconciled.

## How are treats/candy/baskets earned?

### Takeaway
Treats come only from attacks you initiate and win with the final hit; defends, losses and assists give nothing. A Spooky basket ($35, level 5) is the entry requirement, and scary clothing and weapons plus basket tier raise the chance.

### Cited Findings
- Basket: Spooky basket costs $35 at Sally's Sweet Shop, requires level 5, one basket per player — [Spooky wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Spooky), [Torn Index](https://tornindex.com/guide/16512766/)
- Restock: wiki says 1,000 baskets replenish every 15 minutes; an older guide said 5 minutes (possible change) — [Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat), [older guide](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16192843&b=0&a=0rh%3D42)
- Only attacks you start earn treats, not defends; you must win and land the last hit; "Defeats and assists are null" — [2025 guide](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0); the 2024 reference agrees assists give nothing and says the final blow must be made with your spooky weapon for the boost — [2024 One Stop Reference](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=2&t=16424442&b=0&a=0)
- Scary clothing and weapons raise the chance of earning treats; the basket itself gives a percentage bonus — [Torn Stats guide](https://www.tornstats.com/guides/show/48), [Torn Index](https://tornindex.com/guide/16192843/)
- Basket levelling is free and driven by treats earned from attacks; perk upgrades cost treats — [Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat)

### Inferences
- "Last hit" probably means a win outcome with a kill-type result (hospitalize, or mug); whether leave or mug counts is not stated in what I could read. User asked specifically; unresolved.

### Gaps
- Which attack outcomes (leave/mug/hospitalize) count, level or stat restrictions on targets, per-target limits or cooldowns: not found.
- Base treat proc chance and exact effect of costume/mask pieces: not found.

## What do baskets/treats contain or exchange for? Tiers, odds, rewards

### Takeaway
Baskets form a tier ladder with growing treat-chance bonuses, ending at Apocalyptic via the M'aol summon. Treats are exchangeable all at once for cans, candy or alcohol; guides advise exchanging last.

### Cited Findings
- Tier boosts and item IDs: Spooky #920 (base); Creepy #1003 +5%; Frightful #1005 +15%; Horrifying #1009 +35%; Petrifying #1010 +40%; Nightmarish #1011 +45%; Apocalyptic #1448 (boost unknown, under 100%) — [Creepy](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Creepy), [Frightful](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Frightful), [Horrifying](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Horrifying), [Petrifying](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Petrifying), [Nightmarish](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Nightmarish), [Apocalyptic](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Apocalyptic)
- Frightful upgrades to Haunting after 90 total treats; Petrifying is reached from Horrifying at 1,940 total treats; Horrifying is untradeable — [Frightful](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Frightful), [Petrifying](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Petrifying)
- Perk upgrades include Doubler, Tripler, Quadrupler, Quintupler (Quintupler is 1% chance to quintuple), Candyman, Sweet Release, Freebie, Cashback, Mortal Coil, Inflation — [Trick or Treat wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat)
- Treats exchange (all at once, "Exchange All") for cans, candies or alcohol; early returns are low, so finish upgrades before exchanging — [2025 guide](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=61&t=16504610&b=0&a=0)
- M'aol: summoned by a player who has collected 9,999 treats; upgrades a Nightmarish basket to Apocalyptic; purple-tier upgrades (Mortal Coil, Inflation) are advised only for summoning; reward is the basket and server-wide disruption — [M'aol wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/M'aol), [Apocalyptic wiki](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Halloween_Basket_:_Apocalyptic), [Reward thread](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&f=2&t=16429263&b=0&a=0)

### Inferences
- Item IDs above could be used directly by the overlay to detect basket tier from an inventory call.

### Gaps
- Per-upgrade treat costs, exchange rates, merits/honors/medals: not found.
- IDs for Freaky and Haunting baskets: not found.
- What M'aol actually does to players in 2025/2026: not detailed in sources I could read.
