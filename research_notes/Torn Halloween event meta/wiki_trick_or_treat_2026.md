# Trick or Treat wiki page, read from the user's screenshots (page last edited 24 Oct 2026, 21:04)

Source: https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Trick_or_Treat, transcribed from 25 phone screenshots the user sent on 2026-10-25. This supersedes the search-snippet figures in the other notes wherever they disagree.

## Event and timing
- Yearly event: attack players to collect treats and fill a Halloween basket. Category: Annual Event. First added in patch #67 (30/10/18).
- 2026 is confirmed: reference [1] is Sugarvalves, "Don't Be a Hallo-Weenie" (23/10/26).
- Baskets go on sale for everyone at 12:00 TCT on 24 October, regardless of the player's event start time.
- Each player has a selected timeslot. Example from the Mortal Coil/Inflation notes: with an 11 AM timeslot the first hourly proc is 25 Oct 11:00 TCT and the last is 1 Nov 10:00 TCT, i.e. the event runs 7 days from your slot.
- History: in 2020 baskets went on sale at 12:00 TCT and the event began at 14:00 TCT; the event began at 12:00 TCT until flexible start times were added.

## Baskets
- Spooky basket bought at Sally's Sweet Shop; level 5 required. Intro text: 1,000 baskets replenished every 15 minutes. Patch history ("Trick or Treat Draws Close!"): limit changed from 100 every 15 minutes to 1,000 every 5 minutes. (The page contradicts itself; the patch note is the later change.)
- A basket stays on the account forever, does not need rebuying, cannot be traded.
- Basket upgrades automatically. Only treats earned through attacks trigger evolution; treats gained through upgrades do not.

## Treat chance
- Base chance after defeating a player: 35%. Each successive basket upgrade adds 5%, so Nightmarish (9 upgrades) gives 80%.
- Scary clothing: wearing one item boosts the chance; more than one adds nothing. The boost is the "I See Dead People" upgrade (+10%).
- Scary weapon on the final hit boosts the chance: the "Here's Johnny" upgrade (+10%).
- So the maximum is 80% + 10% + 10% = 100% with Nightmarish and both upgrades.
- Treats cannot be lost, even if you are attacked.
- Attacking anyone, active or inactive, gives the same chance.
- Any attack type (leave, mug, hosp, arrest) gives the same chance.
- Multiplier upgrades stack: with Doubler and Tripler there is a 2% chance of 6 treats.

## Spending treats
- Exchange (all at once) for cans, candy or alcohol, or buy upgrades. Both use treats up permanently.
- Base exchange pool: Bag of Candy Kisses, Bag of Tootsie Rolls, Bottle of Beer, Bottle of Pumpkin Brew, Can of Goose Juice, Can of Damp Valley, Can of Crocozade. Upgrades add better items.
